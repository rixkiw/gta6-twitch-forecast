# Data attribution

Model inputs are provided by the contest kit. Viewer and channel counts are
SullyGnome.com hourly Twitch category averages, attributed to
[SullyGnome](https://sullygnome.com). The kit's `input/NOTICE.txt` describes
sampling, provenance and collection. It reports that SullyGnome publishes no
open licence and asks visitors not to scrape its site. This entry performs no
network requests and ships no kit observations. The MIT licence covers this
repository's original software, not the supplied data.

Unsampled hours are absent observations. The supplied launch descriptions,
including future launch dates, are contest scenario metadata; they are not
independently verified news. No external outcomes were researched or used.
